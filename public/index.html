Vogue Paris
1920–1954

Covers from Gallica, Bibliothèque nationale de France, title Vogue (Paris), ISSN 0750-3628. Gallica's run stops in 1954 and skips 1941–44, when Condé Nast suspended the French edition under the Occupation. Some years are bound annual volumes, so one cover stands for the year.
